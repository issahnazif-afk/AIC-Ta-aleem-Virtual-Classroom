# Story Time · Level 1 (ages 4–8): teacher review sheet

Twenty-two original picture stories written for Ta'aleem. The characters (Yusuf, Maryam, Adam, Mama, Baba,
Jiddo, Teta, Bilal, Zara, Omar, Ustadha Huda, Aunty Salma, Uncle Sami) are made up; no Prophet or Companion is ever shown.
Each story ends with one hadith or ayah. The English of each hadith is a simple meaning for young children; please check it.

## The stories, page by page

### 1. The Smile That Travelled (Smiling)

1. Maryam woke up and stretched. The sun was shining, so she smiled a big, sunny smile.
2. At breakfast she smiled at Mama. Mama smiled back. “Assalamu alaykum, my little sunshine!”
3. On the way to the bus, Mama smiled at Aunty Salma, their neighbour. Aunty Salma smiled too.
4. Uncle Sami, the bus driver, looked tired today. Can you help Maryam?
   - *Tap:* Tap Maryam to smile and wave! → Maryam smiled and waved. Uncle Sami smiled a big smile back!
5. At school, Zara sat all alone. Maryam sat next to her and smiled. Soon Zara was smiling too.
6. That evening Baba asked, “Why is everyone so happy today?” Mama laughed. “It all started with one little smile!”

**Question:** What did Maryam give to everyone she met? (**A smile** / A sweet / A balloon)

### 2. Bismillah, Adam! (Table manners)

1. It was dinner time. Mama made rice, and Teta brought her yummy dates.
2. Little Adam was so hungry! He grabbed the food with his left hand, from Yusuf’s plate.
3. “Wait, Adam!” said Yusuf kindly. “First we say Bismillah. Then we eat with our right hand, from the food in front of us.”
4. Adam lifted his right hand high. Can you help him?
   - *Tap:* Tap Adam to say “Bismillah!” with him. → “Bismillah!” said Adam, as loud as he could.
5. Everyone clapped. Adam ate from his own plate with his right hand. He did not spill one grain of rice!
6. When dinner was finished, Jiddo smiled and said, “Alhamdulillah!” And Adam said it too.

**Question:** What do we say before we eat? (Goodbye / **Bismillah** / Hooray)

### 3. Misk Is Thirsty (Kindness to animals)

1. It was a very hot day. Yusuf and Bilal were playing ball in the garden.
2. “Meow…” said a tiny, tired voice. Under the tree sat Misk, the cat, panting in the heat.
3. “Poor Misk is thirsty,” said Yusuf. He ran inside and filled a bowl with cool water.
4. Now Misk needs her water. Where should the bowl go?
   - *Tap:* Tap the bowl to give Misk her water. → Lap, lap, lap! Misk drank and drank.
5. Misk purred and rubbed against Yusuf’s legs. “I think she is saying thank you!” laughed Bilal.
6. Mama smiled from the window. “Allah loves it when we are kind to every living thing, big or small.”

**Question:** How did Yusuf help Misk? (He chased her away / **He gave her water** / He forgot about her)

### 4. The Blue Vase (Telling the truth)

1. Yusuf had a new red ball. “Just one little bounce inside,” he thought. Bounce… bounce…
2. CRASH! The ball hit Mama’s blue vase. It fell down and broke into pieces.
3. Yusuf’s tummy felt funny. “Maybe I can hide the pieces,” he thought. “Or say Misk did it…”
4. But Yusuf knew that Allah sees everything, and that the truth is always best. What should he do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Mama so Yusuf can tell her the truth. → “Mama, I am sorry. I bounced my ball inside, and I broke your vase.”
5. Mama gave Yusuf a big hug. “Thank you for telling me the truth. That makes my heart happy.”
6. They cleaned up together. “Balls are for outside,” said Mama. Yusuf nodded. “In sha Allah, I will remember!”

**Question:** What brave thing did Yusuf do? (He hid the pieces / He said Misk did it / **He told the truth**)

### 5. Salam, Salam, Everywhere (Saying salam)

1. At weekend school, Ustadha Huda said, “Saying salam spreads love between people. Let’s say it to everyone this week!”
2. The next morning Yusuf said “Assalamu alaykum!” to Teta. “Wa alaykum assalam, my dear!” she said with a smile.
3. He said salam to Aunty Salma, watering her flowers, and to Uncle Sami on the bus.
4. At school there was a new boy named Omar. He stood by the door, looking shy.
   - *Tap:* Tap Omar so Yusuf can say salam to him. → “Assalamu alaykum! I’m Yusuf.” Omar smiled. “Wa alaykum assalam! I’m Omar.”
5. Yusuf showed Omar where to hang his coat. At lunch they sat together and shared their dates.
6. By the end of the week, Omar was Yusuf’s good friend. And it all began with one salam!

**Question:** What do we answer when someone says “Assalamu alaykum”? (**Wa alaykum assalam** / Goodnight / Thank you)

### 6. The Last Two Cookies (Sharing)

1. Teta baked her famous date cookies. Mmm! The whole house smelled warm and sweet.
2. Everyone had some. Soon there were only two cookies left on the plate, and Maryam took them both.
3. Then she saw little Adam. He had dropped his cookie, and now he was crying.
4. Maryam thought, “If I were Adam, I would want a cookie too.” What will she do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Maryam to share a cookie with Adam. → “Here, Adam. One for you, and one for me!”
5. Adam stopped crying and gave Maryam a big, crumbly hug.
6. Maryam’s one cookie tasted even sweeter than two. Teta said, “Masha’Allah! You loved for your brother what you love for yourself.”

**Question:** Why did Maryam share her cookie? (She did not like cookies / **She wanted Adam to be happy, like her** / Teta told her to)

### 7. The Stone on the Path (Helping everyone)

1. Every Friday, Yusuf walks to the masjid with Jiddo. Jiddo walks slowly, with his wooden stick.
2. Halfway there, Jiddo’s stick knocked against a big stone in the middle of the path. He nearly tripped!
3. “Someone else might fall over it,” said Yusuf. “A little child, or someone who cannot see well.”
4. Let’s help Yusuf make the path safe.
   - *Tap:* Tap the stone to move it off the path. → Heave-ho! Yusuf rolled the big stone to the side of the path.
5. Jiddo’s eyes twinkled. “Masha’Allah, Yusuf! Moving something harmful out of the way is a charity.”
6. On the way home, Yusuf picked up a banana peel and a sharp can, and put them in the bin. The path was safe for everyone!

**Question:** Why did Yusuf move the stone? (**So that nobody would trip** / To keep it as a toy / To throw it)

### 8. Jazakallahu Khayran, Uncle Sami! (Saying thank you)

1. Every morning, Uncle Sami drives Bilal to school in the big yellow bus. Bilal jumps on and runs to his seat.
2. One rainy day, Bilal forgot his umbrella. Uncle Sami held his own big umbrella over Bilal, all the way to the school door.
3. Bilal stopped. Uncle Sami was wet, but Bilal was dry. What should Bilal say?
   - *Tap:* Tap Uncle Sami so Bilal can thank him. → “Jazakallahu khayran, Uncle Sami!” Uncle Sami smiled from ear to ear.
4. “It means: may Allah reward you with goodness,” Bilal told his friends. Saying thank you made two people happy!
5. That evening Bilal thanked Mama for his lunch, and Baba for fixing his bike. Their faces lit up.
6. At bedtime, Bilal raised his hands. “Alhamdulillah, thank You, Allah, for kind people and for everything You give me.”

**Question:** What can we say to thank someone? (**Jazakallahu khayran** / Go away / Nothing)

### 9. The Angry Volcano (Staying calm)

1. Yusuf built the tallest tower of blocks ever. It was almost as tall as he was!
2. Then little Adam came crawling… and BUMP! The tower fell down.
3. Yusuf felt hot inside, like a bubbling volcano. He wanted to shout and stamp his feet!
4. Baba came and knelt down. “When you feel angry, say A‘udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim. If you are standing, sit down. And drink some water.”
5. Yusuf tried it. Can you help him calm down?
   - *Tap:* Tap Yusuf to take a deep breath with him. → Breathe in… breathe out… “A‘udhu billah.” The volcano went quiet.
6. “Let’s build it again, together,” said Yusuf. Adam handed him the blocks, one by one. The new tower was even taller!

**Question:** What can we do when we feel angry? (Shout and hit / **Say A‘udhu billah, sit down and drink water** / Break things)

### 10. Jiddo’s Slow Steps (Respecting elders)

1. “Race you to the park!” shouted Bilal. The children ran ahead, laughing.
2. But Zara looked back. Jiddo was walking very slowly with his stick, all alone, carrying a heavy bag.
3. Zara wanted to run and play. But she also wanted to help Jiddo. What should she do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Zara to go back and help Jiddo. → “Can I carry your bag, Jiddo? Let’s walk together.”
4. Yusuf and Bilal saw Zara and came running back. They walked with Jiddo, step by slow step.
5. At the park, Jiddo sat under a big tree and told them a story from when he was a little boy.
6. Then Jiddo raised his hands. “May Allah bless you, my kind children.” Their hearts felt as big as the sky.

**Question:** How did the children show respect to Jiddo? (They left him behind / **They carried his bag and walked with him** / They laughed at his slow steps)

### 11. Mama’s Busy Day (Helping our mother)

1. Mama was busy all day. She cooked, she cleaned, and she looked after baby Adam.
2. In the evening, Mama sat down and sighed. “I am so tired,” she said softly.
3. Yusuf and Maryam whispered together. “Let’s help Mama!” What can they do?
   - *Tap:* Tap the children to start helping! → Yusuf set the table. Maryam folded the towels. Then they played with Adam so Mama could rest.
4. Maryam drew a card with flowers and a big heart. It said: “We love you, Mama!”
5. Mama hugged them both. “Masha’Allah! Where did these little helpers come from?”
6. That night Mama made a special dua for them. And Yusuf and Maryam made a dua for Mama too.

**Question:** Who deserves our kindness and help the most? (**Our mother** / Our toys / Nobody)

### 12. The Tap That Sang (Not wasting)

1. Every night, Yusuf brushed his teeth while the tap sang: glug, glug, glug. The water ran and ran.
2. Teta peeked in. “Yusuf, where is all that water going?” “Down the drain!” Yusuf giggled.
3. “Water is a gift from Allah,” said Teta. “Some children walk a long way to get just one bucket. We must not waste it.”
4. The tap is still singing. Can you help Yusuf?
   - *Tap:* Tap the tap to turn it off! → Squeak! Yusuf turned off the tap and used a little cup of water instead.
5. The next day, Yusuf and Maryam gave the water left in their cups to the thirsty flowers.
6. At dinner, Yusuf took a small helping first. “I can always ask for more,” he said. Nothing was wasted, alhamdulillah!

**Question:** How can we save water when we brush our teeth? (Let the tap run / **Turn off the tap and use a cup** / Splash it everywhere)

### 13. Bubble Hands (Keeping clean)

1. Maryam and Adam made mud pies in the garden. Squish, squash! Their hands were brown and sticky.
2. “Lunch is ready!” called Mama. Adam ran inside and reached for the bread with his muddy hands.
3. “Stop!” laughed Maryam. “Muslims love to be clean. First we wash our hands with soap.”
4. Can you help them make lots of bubbles?
   - *Tap:* Tap Adam to wash with soap: scrub, scrub! → Scrub the palms, the backs, between the fingers… Rinse! Sparkling clean!
5. Later, Maryam watched Baba make wudu for salah. Hands, mouth, nose, face, arms, head, ears and feet.
6. Then they prayed together, fresh and clean. “Allah loves those who keep themselves clean,” said Baba.

**Question:** What do we do before we eat? (**Wash our hands** / Play in the mud / Wipe our hands on our clothes)

### 14. A Gift for Aunty Salma (Good neighbours)

1. A new neighbour moved in next door. Her name was Aunty Salma, and she lived all by herself.
2. From the window, Maryam saw her sitting on her step. “Mama, I think Aunty Salma is lonely.”
3. “Let’s welcome her,” said Mama. Together they baked date cookies and put them in a pretty box.
4. They walked next door. Can you knock on the door with Maryam?
   - *Tap:* Tap Maryam to knock on the door: knock, knock! → “Assalamu alaykum, Aunty Salma! Welcome to our street!”
5. Aunty Salma’s eyes shone. She picked flowers from her garden for Maryam to take home.
6. Now Aunty Salma comes for tea every Friday. “A good neighbour is a treasure,” she says.

**Question:** How did Maryam and Mama welcome their new neighbour? (They ignored her / **They brought her a gift and said salam** / They made noise all night)

### 15. Maryam’s Little Seed (Patience)

1. At weekend school, Ustadha Huda gave each child a tiny seed. “Plant it, water it, and be patient,” she said.
2. Maryam planted her seed in a pot by the window, and gave it a little drink of water.
3. The next morning she ran to look. Nothing! Not even a tiny green leaf. “Hmph,” said Maryam.
4. Teta said, “Good things take time. Keep caring for it, and make dua.” So every day, Maryam watered her seed and waited.
5. One, two, three, four, five days… Look at the pot!
   - *Tap:* Tap the pot to see what happened! → A little green sprout! SubhanAllah!
6. Weeks later, Maryam’s plant had a bright yellow flower. “Being patient was worth it!” she smiled.

**Question:** What did Maryam learn? (To give up quickly / **To be patient and keep trying** / That seeds never grow)

### 16. One Ayah at a Time (A little every day)

1. Bilal wanted to learn Surah al-Ikhlas all in one go. He read it again and again and again.
2. Soon his eyes felt heavy, and the words got all mixed up. “I can’t do it!” he said.
3. Jiddo pointed at a little bird by the window. “See how it drinks? A little sip, then another. Let’s learn one ayah each day.”
4. Monday, one ayah. Tuesday, the next one. Wednesday, one more. Can you help Bilal finish on Thursday?
   - *Tap:* Tap Bilal to read the last ayah with him. → Bilal knew all four ayahs! Masha’Allah!
5. At weekend school, Bilal recited the whole surah to Ustadha Huda, without one mistake.
6. “A little every day is the best way,” said Bilal. Now he learns a new ayah every morning, just like the little bird.

**Question:** What is the best way to learn? (**A little bit every day** / Everything in one night / Never)

### 17. Sweet Words (Kind words)

1. Zara drew a picture of a garden with a big yellow sun. She was very proud of it.
2. Omar looked at it and said, “That’s a funny sun. It looks like a pancake!”
3. Zara’s smile went away. Unkind words can hurt, even when they are said as a joke.
4. Yusuf came over. What kind thing could he say?
   - *Tap:* Tap Yusuf to say something kind. → “I love your colours, Zara! Your sun makes me feel warm.”
5. Omar felt sorry. “Zara, I’m sorry. Can you show me how you drew those flowers?”
6. Soon all three were drawing together. Kind words are like flowers: they make everyone’s day brighter.

**Question:** What made Zara smile again? (**A kind word** / A loud noise / Being left alone)

### 18. The Coins in the Jar (Giving)

1. Yusuf was saving coins in a jar. Clink, clink! He wanted to buy a shiny toy car.
2. At the masjid, the imam spoke about children far away who did not have enough food to eat.
3. Yusuf thought about his jar of coins. He thought about the hungry children. He thought very hard.
4. The next Friday, Yusuf took his jar to the masjid. Can you help him?
   - *Tap:* Tap the charity box to put the coins in. → Clink, clink, clink! Yusuf gave his coins to help the hungry children.
5. Baba put his hand on Yusuf’s shoulder. “The hand that gives is better than the hand that takes. I am proud of you.”
6. Yusuf did not get the toy car that week. But his heart felt warm and full, and he knew Allah saw his good deed.

**Question:** Which hand is better, the one that gives or the one that takes? (**The hand that gives** / The hand that takes / Neither)

### 19. Goodnight, Little Adam (Bedtime)

1. It was bedtime, but Adam was bouncing like a ball. “I’m not sleepy!” he said.
2. “Let me show you our bedtime way,” said Maryam. “First, we make wudu and brush our teeth.”
3. “Then we lie down on our right side, and Mama reads the three Quls with us.”
4. Now it is time for the sleeping dua. Can you say it with Adam?
   - *Tap:* Tap Adam to say the dua together. → “Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya.” In Your name, O Allah, I sleep and I wake.
5. Adam yawned a great big yawn. His eyes closed slowly… slowly…
6. Even Misk curled up at the end of the bed. Goodnight, Adam. Goodnight, Misk. Goodnight, moon.

**Question:** What do we do before we sleep? (Jump on the bed all night / **Make wudu, read the Quls and say our dua** / Watch TV)

### 20. Alhamdulillah for the Rain (Being thankful)

1. Yusuf put on his shoes for the park. But then… pitter, patter! It began to rain.
2. “Oh no! Now we can’t go out,” Yusuf grumbled, with his face pressed to the window.
3. Teta sat beside him. “Look, Yusuf. The rain is a gift from Allah. Who is happy about the rain?”
4. Look outside! Tap the flowers and see who is happy.
   - *Tap:* Tap the flowers! → The flowers drink, the trees drink, and the birds have water too. SubhanAllah!
5. Teta taught him the dua for rain: “Allahumma sayyiban nafi‘a.” O Allah, make it a helpful rain.
6. When the rain stopped, Yusuf put on his boots and jumped in every puddle. “Alhamdulillah for the rain!” he laughed.

**Question:** What do we say for all of Allah’s gifts, even the rain? (**Alhamdulillah** / Oh no / Nothing)

### 21. Bilal Lends a Hand (Helping others)

1. It was Omar’s first day at weekend school. He did not know where anything was.
2. Oops! Omar tripped, and his books and pencils went flying everywhere.
3. Some children giggled. But Bilal did not giggle. What will Bilal do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Bilal so he can help Omar. → “Don’t worry, I’ll help you!” Bilal picked up every book and pencil.
4. Bilal showed Omar the coat hooks, the shoe rack and the wudu room. “Stick with me!” he said.
5. In class, Bilal’s work was hard today. Ustadha Huda gave him extra time and helped him, and he finished it all.
6. “You helped Omar today,” said Ustadha Huda, “and Allah helped you. Allah helps us when we help others.”

**Question:** What did Bilal do when Omar dropped his books? (He laughed / **He helped pick them up** / He walked away)

### 22. The Shiniest Heart (A good heart)

1. It was Eid! Zara had new shoes that sparkled in the sun. “Look how shiny they are!” she said.
2. Maryam looked down at her old brown shoes. They had a little scratch. She felt a bit sad.
3. At the masjid door, everyone took off their shoes. Shiny ones, old ones, big ones and tiny ones, all in a row.
4. After the Eid prayer, an old lady could not reach the plate of dates. What can Maryam do?
   - *Tap:* Tap the old lady so Maryam can help her. → Maryam brought her the dates and a cup of water. “Eid Mubarak!”
5. Zara saw it all. “Maryam,” she said, “you didn’t need shiny shoes. You have the shiniest heart!”
6. Teta hugged Maryam. “Allah does not look at our shoes or our clothes. He looks at our hearts and what we do.”

**Question:** What does Allah look at? (Our new shoes / **Our hearts and our deeds** / Our toys)
