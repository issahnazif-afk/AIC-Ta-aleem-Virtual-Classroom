# Reading Library · teacher review sheet

Forty original books written for Al-Huda Islamic Weekend School: twenty First Readers (Level 1, ages 4–6, six pages) and twenty
Adventure Readers (Level 2, ages 7–8, eight pages). The characters are made up; no Prophet or Companion is ever shown.
Each book ends with one hadith or ayah. The English is a simple meaning for young readers; please check it and sign the last column.

## The books, page by page

### Level 1, 1. The Big Red Kite (Taking turns)

1. Yusuf has a big red kite. Up it goes, high in the sky!
2. Maryam looks up. “Can I fly it too?” she asks.
3. Yusuf holds the string tight. It is his kite. Hmm.
4. What should Yusuf do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Yusuf to give Maryam a turn. → “Here, Maryam. Your turn!”
5. Whoosh! The kite dips and zooms. Maryam laughs and laughs.
6. One turn for you, one turn for me. Flying is more fun with two.

**Words:** kite, tree, flowers
**Quiz:** What colour is the kite? **Red** · Who wanted a turn? **Maryam**
**What would you do?** Your friend wants a turn on your swing. What do you do? (**Give them a turn** / Swing all day / Run home)

### Level 1, 2. Splash Goes the Castle (Trying again)

1. Hamza builds a sand castle. Pat, pat, pat.
2. He adds a tall tower and a shell on top.
3. Here comes a wave. SPLASH! The castle is gone.
4. Hamza wants to cry. Then he sees his bucket.
   - *Tap:* Tap the bucket to start again! → “Bismillah. I will try again,” says Hamza.
5. This time he builds it far from the waves. Pat, pat, pat.
6. The new castle is even bigger. Hamza smiles. Easy after hard!

**Words:** shell, bucket, starfish
**Quiz:** What knocked down the castle? **A wave** · What did Hamza say before he tried again? **Bismillah**
**What would you do?** Your tower of blocks falls down. What do you do? (Kick the blocks / **Say Bismillah and build again** / Give up)

### Level 1, 3. Gentle with the Ducks (Being gentle)

1. Bilal and Baba go to the river. Quack, quack!
2. The ducks are hungry. Baba gives Bilal some seeds.
3. A boy runs by. He throws a stone at the ducks!
4. The ducks are scared. What can Bilal do?
   - *Tap:* Tap Bilal to share his seeds. → “Want some seeds? Throw them softly, like this.”
5. Softly, softly. The ducks come back, one by one.
6. “Gentle is better,” says Omar. Quack! say the ducks.

**Words:** bird, stone, boat
**Quiz:** What did Bilal give the ducks? **Seeds** · How should we be with animals? **Gentle**
**What would you do?** You see a little cat. What do you do? (Chase it / Pull its tail / **Stroke it gently**)

### Level 1, 4. One Scarf, Two Friends (Caring for friends)

1. Snow! Safiya and Idris run outside to play.
2. They make a snowman with a carrot nose.
3. Idris shivers. Brrr! He forgot his scarf.
4. Safiya has a long scarf. Can she help?
   - *Tap:* Tap Safiya to share her scarf. → “My scarf is so long. Half for you, half for me!”
5. Now they walk like a train, wrapped in one scarf. Choo, choo!
6. Inside, Mama makes warm milk. Warm hands, warm hearts.

**Words:** snowman, cup, tree
**Quiz:** What did Idris forget? **His scarf** · What did Safiya do? **She shared her scarf**
**What would you do?** Your friend has no pencil. You have two. What do you do? (**Lend one** / Hide them / Say nothing)

### Level 1, 5. Five Little Lights (The five prayers)

1. It is still dark. Baba wakes Yusuf softly. “Fajr time!” One light.
2. The sun is high. Yusuf stops his game for Dhuhr. Two lights.
3. Asr in the garden. Even the birds are quiet. Three lights.
4. The sun goes down. Maghrib! Yusuf rolls out the mats.
   - *Tap:* Tap the mat to get ready for Maghrib. → Four lights! The family prays together.
5. Isha, and the moon is out. Five little lights.
6. Yusuf yawns. “Five prayers every day,” he whispers. “Like five lights.”

**Words:** mat, lantern, moon
**Quiz:** How many prayers are there each day? **Five** · Which prayer is when the sun goes down? **Maghrib**
**What would you do?** It is time to pray, but you are playing. What do you do? (Keep playing / **Pray first, then play** / Hide)

### Level 1, 6. Where Is Adam’s Shoe? (Tidy things)

1. Adam has one shoe. Where is the other one?
2. Is it under the sofa? No. Just a sock and a crumb.
3. Is it in the toy box? No. Just blocks and a car.
4. Look! Something is peeking out of Misk’s basket.
   - *Tap:* Tap the basket to find the shoe! → There it is! Misk was sleeping on it. Alhamdulillah!
5. Mama shows Adam the shoe rack. “Shoes live here, side by side.”
