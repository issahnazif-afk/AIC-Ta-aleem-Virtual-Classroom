## My du'as

Each du'a is split into parts at its natural pauses: the lesson plays one part, then the child says it back,
and each part is its own Voice Studio recording. The last column is the matching item on hisnmuslim.com (used only
after the school has permission): exact, close (one small difference) or longer (our words are inside a longer item).

## Sunnah of the day

- [x] **Eat with your right hand** (Level 1+). Say Bismillah, eat with your right hand, and eat from the food in front of you. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ told a boy: "Say Bismillah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is in front of you."" [Bukhari 5376](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5376).
- [x] **Smile at people** (Level 1+). Give a big smile to your family and friends today. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that your smile to your brother is a charity." [Tirmidhi 1956](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:1956) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **Say salam** (Level 1+). Say "Assalamu alaikum" to everyone you meet today. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Spread salam between you."" [Muslim 54](https://sunnah.com/muslim:54).
- [x] **Say salam to people you know and people you do not know** (Level 2+). Say salam to someone new at school or at the masjid. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ was asked which deeds are best. He said: feeding people, and saying salam to those you know and those you do not know." [Bukhari 12](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:12).
- [x] **Clean your teeth** (Level 1+). Brush your teeth, or use a miswak, before you pray. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that if it were not too hard for his people, he would have told them to use the miswak before every prayer." [Bukhari 887](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:887).
- [x] **Start with the right** (Level 1+). Put on your right shoe first, and wash your right side first in wudu. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ liked to start with the right: putting on his shoes, combing his hair, his wudu, and everything he did." [Bukhari 168](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:168).
- [x] **Sleep on your right side** (Level 1+). Make wudu before bed, then lie down on your right side. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "When you go to bed, make wudu as you do for prayer, then lie down on your right side."" [Bukhari 247](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:247).
- [x] **Drink in three breaths** (Level 2+). When you drink, take a breath outside the cup three times. Hadith: "Anas said that the Prophet ﷺ would breathe three times while drinking." [Muslim 2028](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2028).
- [x] **Sit down to drink** (Level 2+). Sit down before you drink your water or milk. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ told people not to drink standing up." [Muslim 2024](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2024).
- [x] **Help at home** (Level 1+). Help your family at home today: tidy up, lay the table, or carry something. Hadith: "Aishah was asked what the Prophet ﷺ did at home. She said he helped his family, and when it was time to pray he went to the prayer." [Bukhari 676](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:676).
- [x] **Visit someone who is ill** (Level 2+). Visit, call or make du'a for someone who is not well. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Feed the hungry and visit the sick."" [Bukhari 5649](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5649).
- [x] **Be kind to animals** (Level 1+). Give water or food to an animal, or be gentle with a pet. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ told of a man who gave water to a thirsty dog, and Allah forgave him. He said there is a reward for kindness to every living thing." [Bukhari 2363](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2363).
- [x] **Move something harmful out of the way** (Level 1+). Pick up a stone, rubbish or anything that could hurt someone on the path. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that moving something harmful off the road is a part of faith." [Muslim 35](https://sunnah.com/muslim:35).
- [x] **Say something good, or stay quiet** (Level 1+). Today, only say kind and good words. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should say something good or stay quiet."" [Bukhari 6018](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6018).
- [x] **Be good to your neighbours** (Level 2+). Say salam to a neighbour, or share something with them. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that Jibril kept telling him to be good to the neighbour, until he thought the neighbour would inherit." [Bukhari 6014](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6014).
- [x] **Be kind to the young and respect the old** (Level 2+). Be gentle with a younger child and polite to an older person. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "He is not one of us who is not merciful to our young ones and does not respect our old ones."" [Tirmidhi 1919](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:1919) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **Do not get angry** (Level 1+). If you feel angry today, stop, say A'udhu billah, and calm down. Hadith: "A man asked the Prophet ﷺ for advice. He said: "Do not get angry," and said it again and again." [Bukhari 6116](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6116).
- [x] **Make things easy for others** (Level 3+). Help someone with something hard, and be cheerful about it. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Make things easy, and do not make them hard. Give good news, and do not drive people away."" [Bukhari 69](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:69).
- [x] **Want for others what you want for yourself** (Level 2+). Share something you like with a friend or your brother or sister. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself."" [Bukhari 13](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:13).
- [x] **Shake hands when you meet** (Level 2+). Shake hands and say salam when you meet a friend. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that when two Muslims meet and shake hands, they are forgiven before they part." [Abu Dawud 5212](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:5212) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **Do not complain about food** (Level 1+). Eat what you like, and leave what you do not like, without saying it is bad. Hadith: "Abu Hurayrah said that the Prophet ﷺ never found fault with any food. If he liked it he ate it, and if not he left it." [Bukhari 3563](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3563).
- [x] **Say Alhamdulillah after you eat** (Level 1+). When you finish eating or drinking, say Alhamdulillah. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah is pleased with a person who eats and then praises Him for it, and drinks and praises Him for it." [Muslim 2734](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2734).
- [x] **Pray two rak'ahs when you enter the masjid** (Level 3+). When you go into the masjid, pray two rak'ahs before you sit down. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "When one of you enters the masjid, let him pray two rak'ahs before he sits."" [Bukhari 444](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:444).
- [x] **Fast on Mondays** (Level 3+). If you are old enough and your family agrees, try fasting on a Monday. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ was asked about fasting on Monday. He said: "That is the day I was born."" [Muslim 1162](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1162).
- [x] **Give something, even if it is small** (Level 1+). Give a little charity today, even a coin or half a date. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date."" [Bukhari 1417](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1417).
- [x] **Send salawat on Friday** (Level 2+). On Friday, say "Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad" many times. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that Friday is one of the best days, so send many prayers on him on that day." [Abu Dawud 1047](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1047) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **Make du'a for a friend** (Level 2+). Make du'a for a friend or family member without them knowing. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that the du'a of a Muslim for his brother, when he is not there, is answered, and an angel says: "And for you the same."" [Muslim 2732](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2732).
- [x] **Cover your mouth when you yawn** (Level 1+). Put your hand over your mouth when you yawn. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "When one of you yawns, let him hold his hand over his mouth."" [Muslim 2995](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2995).
- [x] **Keep your nails short and clean** (Level 2+). Ask a grown-up to help you cut your nails, and keep them clean. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that cutting the nails is one of the ways of the fitrah, the natural way." [Bukhari 5891](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5891).
- [x] **Be gentle** (Level 2+). Speak softly and touch things gently today. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Gentleness is not in anything except that it makes it beautiful."" [Muslim 2594](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2594).
- [x] **Tell the truth** (Level 1+). Tell the truth all day, even when it is hard. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Truthfulness leads to goodness, and goodness leads to Jannah."" [Bukhari 6094](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6094).
- [x] **Thank people** (Level 1+). Say thank you, or Jazakallahu khayran, to someone who helps you. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever does not thank people has not thanked Allah."" [Abu Dawud 4811](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4811) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **Say a kind word** (Level 1+). Say something kind to make someone happy. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "A good word is a charity."" [Bukhari 2989](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2989).
- [x] **Say SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi** (Level 2+). Say "SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi" as many times as you can today. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says "SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi" a hundred times in a day has his sins wiped away, even if they were like the foam of the sea." [Bukhari 6405](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6405).
- [x] **Learn something about your deen** (Level 1+). Learn one new thing about Islam today, and tell your family. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever walks a path to seek knowledge, Allah makes easy for him a path to Jannah."" [Muslim 2699](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2699).
- [x] **Go to bed early after Isha** (Level 3+). After Isha, get ready for bed instead of staying up late. Hadith: "Abu Barzah said that the Prophet ﷺ did not like sleeping before Isha or talking late after it." [Bukhari 568](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:568).
- [x] **Share your food** (Level 1+). Share your snack with a friend, a brother or a sister. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said that food for one is enough for two, and food for two is enough for four." [Muslim 2059](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2059).
- [x] **Be extra good to your mother** (Level 1+). Do something kind for your mother today, without being asked. Hadith: "A man asked who most deserves his good company. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Your mother," three times, and then: "Your father."" [Bukhari 5971](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5971).
- [x] **Keep yourself clean** (Level 1+). Wash your hands, keep your clothes neat, and make a careful wudu. Hadith: "The Prophet ﷺ said: "Being clean is half of faith."" [Muslim 223](https://sunnah.com/muslim:223).

## Our Prophet ﷺ: his character

- [x] **Mercy**. The Prophet ﷺ was merciful to everyone: children, old people, the poor and animals. Hadith: "He ﷺ said: "Whoever is not merciful will not be shown mercy."" [Bukhari 5997](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5997). Example: "He ﷺ kissed his grandson al-Hasan. A man said he had ten children and had never kissed one of them. The Prophet ﷺ looked at him and said: "Whoever is not merciful will not be shown mercy."" [Bukhari 5997](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5997). Today: Be gentle with someone smaller than you, or with an animal.
- [x] **Truthfulness**. Even before he became a Prophet, the people of Makkah knew he ﷺ always told the truth. Hadith: "He ﷺ said: "Truthfulness leads to goodness, and goodness leads to Jannah."" [Bukhari 6094](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6094). Example: "He ﷺ stood on the hill of as-Safa and called the people of Makkah. He asked: "If I told you that horsemen were coming from behind this valley, would you believe me?" They said: "Yes. We have only ever known you to tell the truth."" [Bukhari 4770](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4770). Today: Tell the truth all day, even if you made a mistake.
- [x] **Generosity**. The Prophet ﷺ loved to give. He gave away what he had to people who needed it. Hadith: "Jabir said: "The Prophet ﷺ was never asked for anything and said no."" [Bukhari 6034](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6034). Today: Share something you like with someone else.
- [x] **Humility**. He ﷺ was the Messenger of Allah, and he still helped with the work at home. Hadith: "Aishah said that at home he ﷺ helped his family, and when it was time to pray he went to the prayer." [Bukhari 676](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:676). Today: Help at home without being asked.
- [x] **Kindness to children**. The Prophet ﷺ was kind and cheerful with children, and he remembered the little things they loved. Hadith: "He ﷺ said: "He is not one of us who is not merciful to our young ones and does not respect our old ones."" [Tirmidhi 1919](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:1919) (Sahih (al-Albani)). Example: "Anas had a little brother called Abu Umayr, who had a small pet bird. When it died, the Prophet ﷺ came to cheer him up and asked him kindly: "O Abu Umayr, what happened to your little bird?"" [Bukhari 6203](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6203). Today: Play kindly with a younger child.
- [x] **Good manners**. He ﷺ had the best manners of all people. Allah says to him in the Qur'an: "And you are of a great character." Hadith: "Anas said: "I served the Prophet ﷺ for ten years, and he never once said to me, Uff!"" [Bukhari 6038](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6038). Today: Do not say "uff" or complain when someone asks you to do something.
- [x] **Kindness to animals**. The Prophet ﷺ taught that Allah rewards us for being kind to animals. Hadith: "He ﷺ told of a man who gave water to a thirsty dog, and Allah forgave him. He said: "There is a reward for kindness to every living thing."" [Bukhari 2363](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2363). Today: Put out water for birds, or care gently for a pet.
- [x] **Smiling**. The Prophet ﷺ met people with a smile. Hadith: "Jarir said: "Every time the Prophet ﷺ saw me, he smiled at me."" [Bukhari 3035](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3035). Today: Smile at everyone you meet today.
- [x] **Patience**. The Prophet ﷺ taught that real patience is staying calm right at the moment something sad happens. Hadith: "He ﷺ said: "Patience is at the first moment of the hurt."" [Bukhari 1283](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1283). Today: When something goes wrong, stay calm and say "Inna lillah".
- [x] **Gratitude**. The Prophet ﷺ prayed at night until his feet were swollen, to thank Allah. Hadith: "Aishah asked him why he prayed so long. He ﷺ said: "Should I not be a thankful servant?"" [Bukhari 4837](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4837). Today: Thank Allah for three things before you sleep.
- [x] **Courage**. The Prophet ﷺ was the bravest of people. One night the people of Madinah heard a frightening noise. When they went out, he ﷺ was already coming back, having checked that all was safe. Hadith: "Anas said the Prophet ﷺ came back on a horse and told them: "Do not be afraid, do not be afraid."" [Bukhari 2908](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2908). Today: Be brave: try something good that feels hard.
- [x] **Fairness**. The Prophet ﷺ was fair to everyone, and he taught parents to be fair to their children. Hadith: "He ﷺ said: "Fear Allah, and be fair between your children."" [Bukhari 2587](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2587). Today: Take turns fairly and share equally in your games.

## His life ﷺ

- [x] **Born in Makkah, on a Monday**: The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born in Makkah. When he was asked about fasting on Mondays, he said: "That is the day I was born." [Muslim 1162](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1162).
- [x] **An orphan, looked after by Allah**: His father died before he was born, and his mother when he was a little boy. Allah reminds him in the Qur'an that He looked after him. Qur'an 93:6.
- [x] **A shepherd**: As a young man he looked after sheep. He ﷺ said that every Prophet had been a shepherd, and that he looked after sheep for the people of Makkah. [Bukhari 2262](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2262).
- [x] **The first revelation**: He ﷺ used to go to the cave of Hira, on a mountain near Makkah, to worship Allah alone. There the angel Jibril came to him with the first words of the Qur'an: "Read, in the name of your Lord who created." [Bukhari 3](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3). Qur'an 96:1.
- [x] **Calling the people of Makkah**: He ﷺ climbed the hill of as-Safa and called the people to worship Allah alone. They said they had only ever known him to tell the truth. [Bukhari 4770](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4770).
- [x] **The Hijrah: the cave of Thawr**: He ﷺ left Makkah for Madinah with Abu Bakr. They hid in a cave, and he said to his friend: "Do not be sad. Allah is with us." [Bukhari 3653](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3653). Qur'an 9:40 (words 19–23).
- [x] **Building the masjid in Madinah**: In Madinah he ﷺ built a masjid, and he carried the bricks with his Companions. [Bukhari 428](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:428).
- [x] **Returning to Makkah**: Years later he ﷺ returned to Makkah and cleared the idols from around the Ka'bah, reciting: "The truth has come, and falsehood has gone." [Bukhari 4287](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4287).
- [x] **He ﷺ returned to Allah**: He ﷺ passed away in Madinah when he was sixty-three. He left us the Qur'an and his Sunnah, and we send salawat on him. [Bukhari 3536](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3536).

## Special days (Hijri calendar)

- [x] **Jumu'ah (Friday)**: The best day of the week. We have a bath, wear clean clothes, go to the Jumu'ah prayer and send many salawat on the Prophet ﷺ. [Muslim 854](https://sunnah.com/muslim:854).
- [x] **Monday and Thursday**: The Prophet ﷺ liked to fast on Mondays and Thursdays. He said that deeds are shown to Allah on these days. [Tirmidhi 747](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:747) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **The white days**: The 13th, 14th and 15th of every Islamic month, when the moon is full and bright. The Prophet ﷺ taught us to fast these three days. [Tirmidhi 761](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:761) (Hasan Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **The Islamic New Year**: Muharram is the first month. The Prophet ﷺ called it the month of Allah, and said the best fasting after Ramadan is in Muharram. [Muslim 1163](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1163).
- [x] **Ashura**: The 10th of Muharram. The Prophet ﷺ fasted on this day and said that Allah forgives the year before for it. [Muslim 1162](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1162).
- [x] **Ramadan**: The month of fasting, when the Qur'an was first sent down. We fast, pray, read the Qur'an and give charity. Qur'an 2:185 (words 1–12).
- [x] **The last ten nights of Ramadan**: Laylat al-Qadr is better than a thousand months. The Prophet ﷺ told us to look for it in the odd nights of the last ten. [Bukhari 2017](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2017). Qur'an 97:3.
- [x] **Eid al-Fitr**: The Eid after Ramadan, a day of joy. The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah has given us two days of celebration: Eid al-Adha and Eid al-Fitr. [Abu Dawud 1134](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1134) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
- [x] **The first ten days of Dhul-Hijjah**: The best days of the year for good deeds, the Prophet ﷺ said. Say "Allahu akbar" often and do extra good. [Bukhari 969](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:969).
- [x] **The Day of Arafah**: The day the pilgrims stand at Arafah. The Prophet ﷺ said that fasting this day wipes away the sins of the year before and the year after. [Muslim 1162](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1162).
- [x] **Eid al-Adha**: The Eid of the sacrifice, remembering Prophet Ibrahim. A day of prayer, family and sharing meat with the poor. [Abu Dawud 1134](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1134) (Sahih (al-Albani)).
