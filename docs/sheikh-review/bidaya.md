# The Sheikh: candidate passages from Ibn Kathir for review

- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/39 (bx-khuluq, established) — his character was the Qur’an — [Saʿd ibn Hisham said:] I asked ʿAʾisha, the Mother of the Believers: “Tell me about the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.” She said: “Do you not read the Qur’an?” I said: “Yes, I do.” She said: “His character was the Qur’an.”
  - Arabic: سألت عائشة أم المؤمنين فقلت: أخبريني عن خلق رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم، فقالت: أما تقرأ القرآن؟ قلت: بلى، فقالت: كان خلقه القرآن
  - Note: Ibn Kathir gives the chain to Saʿd ibn Hisham; the hadith is in Sahih Muslim (746).
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/40 (bx-khuluq-meaning, established) — what “his character was the Qur’an” means — This means that whatever the Qur’an commanded him, he carried out, and whatever it forbade him, he left.
  - Arabic: ومعنى أنه عليه السلام مهما أمره به القرآن امتثله، ومهما نهاه عنه تركه.
  - Note: Ibn Kathir’s own explanation. The edition prints “ومعنى أنه”.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/40 (bx-muminun, established) — his character was the Qur’an: the first ten ayat of al-Muʾminun — [Zayd ibn Babanus said:] We said to ʿAʾisha: “Mother of the Believers, what was the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ like?” She said: “The character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was the Qur’an.” Then she said: “Do you read Surat al-Muʾminun? Read: ‘Successful indeed are the believers’, up to the tenth ayah.” She said: “That is how the character of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was.”
  - Arabic: قلنا لعائشة يا أم المؤمنين كيف كان خلق رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم؟ قالت: كان خلق رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم القرآن ثم قالت: أتقرأ سورة المؤمنين؟ اقرأ: قد أفلح المؤمنون، إلى العشر. قالت: هكذا كان خلق رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم
  - Note: From al-Bayhaqi with his chain; Ibn Kathir adds that al-Nasaʾi narrated it. The edition names the narrator “زيد بن بابنوس” (other sources: Yazid). The words of 23:1 are Pickthall’s.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/40 (bx-easier, established) — he chose the easier way — [ʿAʾisha said:] Whenever the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was given a choice between two things, he took the easier of them, as long as it was not a sin; if it was a sin, he was the furthest of all people from it. And he never took revenge for himself, unless something Allah made sacred was violated; then he would take revenge for Allah’s sake.
  - Arabic: ما خير رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم بين أمرين إلا أخذ أيسرهما ما لم يكن إثما فإن كان إثما كان أبعد الناس منه، وما انتقم لنفسه إلا أن تنتهك حرمة الله فينتقم لله بها
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim (from Malik).
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/42 (bx-speech, established) — he never insulted, cursed or spoke rudely — [Anas ibn Malik said:] The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was not one who insulted people, nor one who cursed, nor one who spoke rudely.
  - Arabic: لم يكن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم سبابا ولا لعانا ولا فاحشا
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: narrated by Ahmad and al-Bukhari. The rest of the sentence (“كان يقول لأحدنا عند المعاتبة: ماله تربت جبينه”, his mild words of reproach) is left out.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/42 (bx-errand, established) — he laughed with young Anas, who had not yet run his errand — [Anas said:] The Messenger of Allah ﷺ had the best character of all people. One day he sent me on an errand, and I said: “By Allah, I will not go,” though in my heart I meant to go where the Messenger of Allah ﷺ had told me. I went out until I passed some boys playing in the market, and suddenly the Messenger of Allah ﷺ took hold of the back of my neck from behind. I looked at him, and he was laughing. He said: “Unays, did you go where I told you?” I said: “Yes, I am going, O Messenger of Allah.”
  - Arabic: كان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم من أحسن الناس خلقا فأرسلني يوما لحاجة فقلت: والله لا أذهب - وفي نفسي أن أذهب لما أمرني به رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم - فخرجت حتى أمر على صبيان وهم يلعبون في السوق فإذا رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قد قبض بقفاي من ورائي قال: فنظرت إليه وهو يضحك فقال: يا أنيس ذهبت حيث أمرتك؟ فقلت: نعم أنا أذهب يا رسول الله.
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: from Sahih Muslim (ʿIkrima ibn ʿAmmar from Ishaq). “Unays” is the loving diminutive of Anas.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/42 (bx-nine-years, established) — nine years with Anas and never a harsh word — Anas said: By Allah, I served him for nine years, and I never knew him to say about something I had done, “Why did you do this and that?”, or about something I had left, “Why did you not do this and that?”
  - Arabic: قال أنس: والله لقد خدمته تسع سنين ما علمته قال لشئ صنعته لم صنعت كذا وكذا أو لشئ تركته هلا فعلت كذا وكذا
  - Note: Continues bx-errand (Sahih Muslim). Other reports in the same place say ten years.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/43 (bx-abu-umayr, established) — he played with little Abu ʿUmayr and asked about his bird — [Anas said:] The Messenger of Allah ﷺ had the best character of all people. I had a little brother called Abu ʿUmayr (the narrator said: I think he said, just weaned). Whenever the Messenger of Allah ﷺ came and saw him, he would say: “Abu ʿUmayr, what did the little bird do?” It was a little bird he used to play with.
  - Arabic: كان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أحسن الناس خلقا وكان لي أخ يقال له أبو عمير ، قال: أحسبه قال: فطيما، قال: فكان إذا جاء رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فرآه قال: أبا عمير ما فعل النغير، قال نغر كان يلعب به
  - Note: From Ahmad; Ibn Kathir: narrated by all the six books except Abu Dawud. Same event as Bukhari 6129 already in src/data/sheikh-hadith.json.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/52 (bx-salam-children, established) — he greeted children with salam — [Anas narrated] that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ passed by some children who were playing, and he greeted them with salam.
  - Arabic: أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم مر على صبيان يلعبون فسلم عليهم
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: narrated by al-Bukhari, and by Muslim another way. Same hadith as Muslim 2168 already in src/data/sheikh-hadith.json.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 8/38 (bx-usama, established) — he hugged Usama and al-Hasan and prayed for them — [Usama ibn Zayd said:] The Prophet ﷺ used to take me and sit me on his thigh, and sit al-Hasan on his other thigh. Then he would hold us both close and say: “O Allah, have mercy on them, for I have mercy on them.”
  - Arabic: كان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم يأخذني فيقعدني على فخذه ويقعد الحسن على فخذه الأخرى ثم يضمنا ثم يقول: اللهم ارحمهما فإني أرحمهما
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: narrated by al-Bukhari. In vol. 8 (biography of Usama ibn Zayd).
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/46 (bx-repeat, established) — he repeated his words three times so they were understood — [Anas narrated] that when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ spoke, he would repeat a word three times so that it would be understood from him.
  - Arabic: أن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم كان إذا تكلم يعيد الكلمة ثلاثا لتعقل عنه
  - Note: Ibn Kathir quotes al-Tirmidhi: “hasan sahih gharib”. Close to Bukhari 95 (6244 in src/data/sheikh-hadith.json is related).
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/43 (bx-ramadan, established) — he went over the Qur’an with Jibril in Ramadan — [Ibn ʿAbbas said:] The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was the most generous of people, and he was at his most generous in Ramadan, when Jibril met him and went over the Qur’an with him. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was more generous with good than the wind that is sent out.
  - Arabic: كان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أجود الناس، وكان أجود ما يكون في رمضان حين يلقاه جبريل فيدارسه القرآن، فلرسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم أجود بالخير من الريح المرسلة
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: established in the two Sahihs.
- [ ] Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/66 (bx-ibn-masud, established) — he loved to hear the Qur’an recited by others, and wept — [ʿAbdullah ibn Masʿud said:] The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Recite to me.” I said: “Shall I recite to you, when it was sent down to you?” He said: “I love to hear it from someone else.” So I recited Surat al-Nisaʾ until I reached: “But how (will it be with them) when We bring of every people a witness, and We bring thee (O Muhammad) a witness against these?” [ayah 41]. He said: “That is enough.” I turned, and his eyes were flowing with tears.
  - Arabic: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: اقرأ علي، فقلت: اقرأ عليك وعليك أنزل؟ فقال: إني أحب أن أسمعه من غيري، قال: فقرأت سورة النساء حتى بلغت: * (فكيف إذا جئنا من كل أمة بشهيد وجئنا بك على هؤلاء شهيدا) * [الآية: 41] قال: حسبك، فالتفت فإذا عيناه تذرفان
  - Note: Ibn Kathir: narrated by al-Bukhari. The ayah (4:41) is given in Pickthall’s words. The Arabic contains Qur’an text in the edition’s spelling (unvowelled), not the Tanzil text.
- [ ] ⚠ Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/65 (bx-tartil, established) — he recited a surah so slowly it became longer than a longer one — She said: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would recite a surah, reciting it slowly and clearly (tartil), until it became longer than a surah longer than it.
  - Arabic: قالت: وكان رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم يقرأ السورة فيرتلها حتى تكون أطول من أطول منها
  - Note: Doubtful attribution: in this place “she” is ʿAʾisha (the paragraph begins “قالت عائشة”), but in Sahih Muslim (733) these words are from Hafsa. Ibn Kathir summarises without chains here. Please check before use.
- [ ] ⚠ Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa'l-Nihaya 6/317 (bx-jubayr, reported) — the most beautiful voice; he recited with tartil as Allah commanded — Jubayr ibn Mutʿim said: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ recited al-Tin wa’l-Zaytun in the sunset prayer, and I never heard a voice more pleasing than his ﷺ. And he used to recite with tartil, as Allah, Mighty and Majestic, commanded him.
  - Arabic: قال جبير بن مطعم: قرأ رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم في المغرب بالتين والزيتون، فما سمعت صوتا أطيب من صوته صلى الله عليه وسلم، وكان يقرأ ترتيلا كما أمره الله عز وجل بذلك
  - Note: Doubtful, proposed as “reported”: in al-Bukhari and Muslim the words about al-Tin and the beautiful voice are from al-Baraʾ ibn ʿAzib; Jubayr ibn Mutʿim reported hearing al-Tur. The passage is in a comparison of the Prophet ﷺ with Dawud (quoting Ibn Hamid), and its last clause is commentary.
